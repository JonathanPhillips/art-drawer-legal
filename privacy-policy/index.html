The Art Drawer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

The Art Drawer is a local-only app for keeping your children's artwork. Everything you add (scans, photos, children's names and birth dates, titles and notes) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud sync. The app itself makes no network connections, and we do not operate a server. Nothing you add is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Camera & photos

Camera access lets you scan artwork. When you pick an existing photo, the system photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose. The app keeps its own resized copy with location and device metadata removed; photos in your library are never changed.

Exports & backups

When you export a backup, a zip of images, or a yearbook PDF, the app hands the file to the system share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, email, a cloud drive, AirDrop) is your choice and is never seen by us. Your device may also include app data in its own backups (iCloud or Google), under Apple's or Google's terms.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no personal or payment information from them, and the unlock status is stored only on your device.

Analytics & tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. We have no way to see how you use the app or what you store in it.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual pieces at any time, or erase everything at once from Settings > Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes all of its data, since nothing is stored anywhere else.

Children

The Art Drawer is meant to be used by parents and caregivers. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children: the names, birth dates, and artwork you enter never leave your device unless you export them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue or use the contact listed on the App Store product page.
