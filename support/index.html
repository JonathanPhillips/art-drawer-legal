The Art Drawer: Support

A private home for your kids' artwork. Everything stays on your phone.

Backing up and moving to a new phone

Settings > Export backup creates a zip with all your pieces, children, books, and full-size scans. Save it to Files or a drive you trust. On the new phone, install The Art Drawer and use Settings > Import backup. Because the app has no account or cloud, this backup is the only way to move everything.

Sharing with family

Settings > Export images makes a plain zip of JPEGs named by child, date, and title, ready for grandparents or a shared family drive. Books makes a printable yearbook PDF.

Free pieces and the unlock

The first 20 pieces are free. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited pieces; there is no subscription. If you reinstall or get a new phone, tap Restore next to the free-pieces count in Settings.

Permissions

  • Camera: scanning artwork.

Picking existing photos uses the system picker, which needs no permission.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete All Data erases everything on the device. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue. Please don't post photos of your children's artwork or their names.
